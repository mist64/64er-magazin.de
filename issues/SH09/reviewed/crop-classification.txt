# HOW THE BUCKETS WERE DECIDED
#   c/     150 dpi colour, 25 %.  Every colour photograph (8701 precedent: every photograph is c/).
#          6-0, 6-1a..6-5b, 13-1..13-5, 25-0, 30-0.  Pages 6-25 and the p30/31 lead are printed in four colours.
#   gray/  150 dpi grey, 25 %.  Black-and-white halftones.  3-0 (the cover as printed on p3, which is black and
#          white there: mean chroma 11, paper tone only), 79-1 (screen photo, white on dark), 92-x1..92-x5
#          (book cover photos, chroma 8-10, paper tone only).
#   bw/    600 dpi bilevel.  Line art, screens with text, the hardcopy.
#          30-3, 140-1..140-5, 113-1 (screen photo of a text mask; bilevel, bw and dots render alike),
#          107-1 (dot-matrix hardcopy of »Catalog Printer+«), 140-6 (the board is a coarse screen that IS the
#          drawing; bw and dots render alike, bw is the plain path).
#          30-1, 30-2, 30-7, 30-8 AGAINST the grey-ground default: the tint is not a ground here, it CARRIES
#          MEANING.  30-1 grey faces = used memory, white = »nicht benutzt«; 30-2 grey/white buffer faces;
#          30-7 grey = LEERRAUM; 30-8 grey wedges against white sectors.  dots/ erases the tint and with it the
#          distinction; bw/ keeps it as a stipple.  Rendered both ways and looked.
#   dots/  600 dpi bilevel, -lat 41x41.  Line art on a PRINTED GREY GROUND (a box tint, no meaning):
#          30-4, 30-5, 30-9, 142-1..142-4.  Rendered 30-9 and 142-1 both ways: dots clears the ground, bw leaves
#          a dot field.
