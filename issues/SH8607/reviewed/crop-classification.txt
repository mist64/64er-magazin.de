# HOW THE FOUR BUCKETS WERE DECIDED (the question a later build will ask):
#   dots/  600 dpi bilevel, halftone screen removed (-lat 41x41).
#          A drawing or screen photo sitting on a PRINTED GREY GROUND. A plain
#          threshold turns that ground into a dot field that survives JBIG2 as
#          noise; -lat 41x41 reads it as ground and drops it.
#   bw/    600 dpi bilevel, pixel dimensions unchanged.
#          Line art, listings, tables, screen shots of text -- black on white
#          with no continuous tone and no grey ground.
#   gray/  150 dpi 8-bit greyscale, 25 %. Geometry shrinks 4x.
#          A real halftone: a photograph, or art with continuous tone that a
#          threshold would destroy.
#   c/     150 dpi colour. Nothing in SH8607 needed it.
# The four that were uncertain between bw/ and dots/ (110-3, 164-1, 6-3, 6-4)
# were settled BY RENDERING both ways and looking, not by argument: all four
# are bw/ -- what looked like a ground is the face's own weight.
