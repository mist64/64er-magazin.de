## How the leads were found

Measured 2026-11-15, after step 300 and before 325, over every article in `issues/` except SH10 itself (27 of ours against 2195; scripts and full output: `/Users/mist/DNB/SH10/tmp/r330/step0.py`, `step0.txt`, per-block attribution `blocks.py`, `blocks.txt`, `blocks6_low.txt`). Text taken with `r330_reprint_compare.py`'s own extractor, so `<pre>`/`<code>`, comments, `futureteufelchen` and `nachdruck` are excluded and line-end hyphens joined the way the diff will join them; words lower-cased.

- **whole article**: 8-word shingles. `ours` = shared / our shingles, `theirs` = shared / its shingles (the merged-reprint signal), `min` = shared / the smaller set. Whole-article threshold ~0.5.
- **per block**: a block of ours matches when at least 50 % of its 8-shingles occur in the candidate; `blk` = matching blocks / our scorable blocks, `run` = longest run of consecutive matching blocks, `strong` = blocks at 80 % or more. Every block match was read.
- **table cells**: identical `<td>`/`<th>` texts (at least 6 chars, not bare numbers).

Direction: SH10 is Sonderheft 10/86 (`64er.issue`). 8612 is younger, and says so: `8612/40 Hardware-Erweiterungen` lists a design »als Bauanleitung im 64'er Sonderheft 10/86«. Every other candidate below is 8607 or older.
