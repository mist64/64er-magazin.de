The X scroll is bits 0-2 (bit 3 is the 38/40-column bit, described in the next item, so the print itself overlaps), and an X shift is horizontal. REASONED from the VIC-II register map, recalled rather than cited; route 2 (series on the page: "Bit 3" has its own entry) supports the 0-2 half. | c07
