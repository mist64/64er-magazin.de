SH8606 — lead-image prefix check (r080, cf729c59 / d8b540a5), done 2026-10-17
==============================================================================

Why this record exists: SH8608's article 94 shipped without its printed prefix
"Text-Manager", set white out of a grey band between the lead drawing and the
headline. OCR returned no block for it (reversed display type does not survive
binarisation) and the lead crop stopped at the drawing's frame above the band,
so the prefix was in neither text nor image and no check could see it. The
owner found it by reading the page. SH8606 is published as 9b444570 and had
not had this look. "h1 does not contain toc_title" is NOT a detector (it flags
12 of SH8606's 19 articles with a toc_title; 122, 134 and 155 are shaped like
the real case and print no prefix), so this was done by eye on the 600 dpi
masters. A check that found nothing is the one most likely to be repeated;
this file says it was done, on which pages, and what was seen.

Method
------
1. A 1/6-scale overview of the FIRST PAGE OF ALL 27 ARTICLES, not only the
   nine with a placed -0 crop: a lead image can be on the page without having
   been cropped, and a crop is only where someone already looked (108's
   drawing, cropped as 108-0 and since removed, was never a lead at all).
   Each page was classified lead image / no lead image from the page.
2. For every page that prints a lead image, a strip from the top of that image
   down to the first body line, cut from masters600/NNN.png at 600 dpi and
   read at 1/3 scale (200 dpi).
3. Everything printed in the strip checked against the h1, the p.intro and the
   figcaption in the article's HTML (grep: 1 hit each).
